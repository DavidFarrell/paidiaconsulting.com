# Workshop welcome email: Build your AI product factory (first cohort, 20 November 2026)

Date: Sunday 25 October 2026
Question: What does someone get when they book, so they arrive ready to build?
Status: Draft by Nikki's Claude at Nikki's request, modelled on the shape of a Maven course's enrolment email (confirm, what you will do, the date, three things to do before, what you need on your machine, what you do not need, sign-off). Rewritten for our format and audience. Not sent. David to make it his.

Subject: You are booked: Build your AI product factory, 20 November

Hi [first name],

You have a place on Build your AI product factory. We are looking forward to building with you.

Over two 90-minute sessions you will take one task you do every week and get AI doing it in stages, with a person checking before anything leaves. You will learn how to say what you want once, so the AI follows it every time, how to check what it produces, and how to improve it as it meets your real work.

The sessions are Friday 20 November and [26] November 2026, 2pm to 3.30pm UK time, online, with a 45-minute drop-in session a week or two later. Your joining link and calendar invitations are attached [or: follow in a separate email].

Before session 1, three things:

1. **Pick the task and gather three real examples.** Something you do every week that starts with reading: emails, forms, reports, a spreadsheet. Bring three real inputs, with anything confidential taken out.
2. **Reply to this email with the task in a line.** We will say whether it fits, and suggest a better one if not.
3. **Have your AI tool open and working** on the computer you will use: ChatGPT, Claude, Microsoft Copilot or Gemini, whichever your organisation allows. A paid plan makes the sessions easier. If you have never used one, say so in your reply and we will get you set up.

You do not need a finished plan or any technical background. If you can write a clear email, you can build a chain of AI steps.

Between the two sessions, plan about an hour to run your first step on real work and note what it gets wrong. That list is your material for session 2. After session 2 there is a 45-minute drop-in session, a week or two later, to fix what breaks when it meets your real week.

If anything is unclear, reply to this email and David answers.

See you on the 20th,

David [and Nikki]
