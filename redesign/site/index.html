A note from David

You have tried it. Nothing stuck.

Dr David Gérouville-FarrellFounder. Fifteen years a university lecturer. Built the UK Government’s first public AI service, for the FCDO.

You opened ChatGPT or Copilot, asked it something, got an answer that was fine and not much use, and closed the tab. Most of the people I meet have had a look and quietly left. Deloitte asked 25,000 UK workers this year: most have used it, the use is mostly searching and drafting, and about half have had no training at all.

It is not because you are slow. It is because nobody has shown you how to use it on your own work.

What you have probably been offered

  • A licence and a webinar.An hour for everyone at once. Six weeks later it is a tab you do not open.
  • A clever thing someone built.Handed over and walked away from. Six months later nobody can explain it, so nobody trusts it.

Neither makes you better at your job. BCG surveyed more than 10,000 workers in 2025 and found regular use is sharply higher among people who had five or more hours of training, and higher again when it was in person, with a coach. Read BCG’s findings

That is the part we do. We build AI that runs in production, for the Foreign Office among others, and we teach people on their own tasks while we build. Our measure of success is that you no longer need us.
