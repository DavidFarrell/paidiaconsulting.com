What we have built

Eight engagements across government, health, charity, education and finance, each told the same way: what they were trying to do, what they had tried, what we did, and what happened. If one of them sounds like you, that is a good sign.

Case studies

1
Great Ormond Street Hospital Charity
70% adoption

From first experiments to everyday use in twelve weeks

Adoption went from zero to 70% in twelve weeks. The CTO: "this isn't change management, it's EQ and IQ."

The job
Get a whole charity, not just the keen few, using AI in their everyday work.
Why then
The tools were already paid for and in people's hands, and the senior team wanted them used, not admired.
What they had tried
One-off sessions and recordings. People liked them on the day and weeks later could not rebuild what they had learnt.
What we did
One-to-one coaching with the senior team, engineering on the priority workflows, and a weekly AI Lab where teams designed their own assistants on their own tasks.
2
FCDO, with Caution Your Blast
£3.6M saved

The UK Government's first public-facing AI service

A five-second reply at 95% accuracy in production, above the human agent baseline. The FCDO estimates £3.6M saved in staff time over five years. Winner of the Digital Leaders AI Excellence Award 2025 and a case study in the UK Government's AI Use Case Library.

The job
Answer the consular enquiries of 500,000 citizens a year, reliably, in every embassy.
Why then
Replies were taking ten days through an email queue.
What they had tried
Human agents working the queue.
What we did
Designed, built and deployed the AI service, with Caution Your Blast on service design and delivery. Live on GOV.UK and on every British Embassy website.

Architecture, build and deployment by Paidia; service design and delivery with Caution Your Blast.

3
Awin
2 hours

Publisher matching in two hours, not ten days

Matching runs in two hours. The placement checks gave people back full days of work.

The job
Match 1,500 vendors to publishers, and check ad placements, without people losing days to it.
What they had tried
Doing it by hand: ten days per matching run.
What we did
A matching tool and automated ad-placement checks. We coached the engineers on building AI that does multi-step work, reviewed the security of connecting AI to their systems, and rolled out Claude Code safely.
4
NHS Midlands, with Hudson and Hayes
11 boards

One diagnostics discovery across eleven Integrated Care Boards

The business case secured funding, and a working prototype followed within weeks.

The job
Agree where AI could help with diagnostics across eleven boards, and get it funded.
What they had tried
Forty-one candidate opportunities and no shared way to choose between them.
What we did
Workshops that narrowed the forty-one to a prioritised shortlist, and a model showing how a 1% cut in missed appointments could free roughly 90,000 diagnostic appointments a year.

Discovery and business case with Hudson and Hayes.

5
Pearson
FTSE 100

A platform strategy and architecture for a FTSE 100 education company

The job
Give learners a single front door, in support of Pearson's early-careers ambitions, including young people not in education, employment or training.
What they had tried
Separate delivery systems that AI assistants could not work across.
What we did
A platform strategy and an architecture that lets AI assistants work across those systems through a standard way of connecting them.
6
Rimes, with Hudson and Hayes
50 engineers

A roadmap and the tooling to use it

The job
Triple the business, with a fifty-strong engineering team and legacy systems AI could not reach.
What we did
An AI transformation roadmap, a standard way to make legacy systems reachable by AI, and a developer-tooling programme with onboarding and process-design workshops.

Roadmap with Hudson and Hayes.
