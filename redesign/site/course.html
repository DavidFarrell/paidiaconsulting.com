What arrives, day by day

  1. Day 1
    Behind the curtainWhat AI actually does: it learns patterns from past data and uses them to predict. Long before ChatGPT it was already forecasting numbers, sorting things into piles and learning how work gets done.
  2. Day 2
    Every AI has edgesA system can only give the answers it was built to give, and only make things like what it has seen. Why an image tool struggles to draw a single grape, and why bias arrives with the data.
  3. Day 3
    What your prompt really doesA language model predicts the next word. What you type steers where it goes, so context is everything. The same steering is how people trick it.
  4. Day 4
    Rewrite anything for anyoneChange the tone, the audience, the length or the language: for your boss, for a beginner, in German. The effort of tailoring a message goes, and you check what matters.
  5. Day 5
    Read less, understand moreSummarise a long document, then ask it questions until you understand it, at your own pace and on your own terms.
  6. Day 6
    From inbox to systemPull the facts out of emails and into the place they belong. Checking the AI's work is quicker than doing the job yourself, so a person stays in the loop.
  7. Day 7
    The limits of trustWhy AI makes things up, why it has a knowledge cut-off, and how to decide what needs checking. Treat it as a language tool, not a database.
